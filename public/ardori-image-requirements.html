General Photography Guidelines

🎨 Lighting & Tone

  • Warm, natural lighting for all lifestyle shots
  • Even studio lighting for product-only shots (no harsh shadows)
  • Color temperature: warm neutral (5000–5500K)
  • Avoid cool/blue tones — the brand is earth-toned and organic

🎨 Brand Color Palette Reference

  • #FAF8F5 — Off-white (primary background)
  • #F5F2ED — Warm off-white (secondary background)
  • #121B2D — Deep navy
  • #1A2332 — Dark navy
  • #C9A86C — Gold accent
  • #2C2824 — Warm dark brown

📐 Product Photography Standards

  • Minimum 5–7 angles per product: Front, Back, Side, Detail (stitching/hardware), Interior, Strap, Styled Angle
  • Product fills 70–80% of frame with consistent 10–15% breathing margin
  • Background: off-white to light beige (not pure white)
  • All product shots must work at 1.5× zoom (hover-to-zoom feature)
  • Second alternate-angle image needed for hover state on product cards

🔲 Safe Margin Rules for Text Overlay Images

  • Hero images: Keep center 40% width × 50% height clear of key subjects
  • Section backgrounds (navy patterns): No focal subjects — even decorative distribution
  • Care guide images: No text overlay — full creative freedom

📁 File Format & Delivery

  • Photos: JPG, sRGB color profile, quality 85–90%
  • Patterns/Logos: PNG with transparency where applicable
  • Video: MP4, H.264, 1080p minimum, loop-friendly (seamless first/last frame)
  • Naming convention: Use descriptive kebab-case names (e.g., care-daily.jpg, values-lifestyle-1.jpg)
